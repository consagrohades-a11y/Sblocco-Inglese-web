# E. Anti-spam operating rules

## Before posting

1. **Read the group rules every time.** Do not assume a group allows links because another one did.
2. Do not post where commercial promotion is prohibited.
3. If promotion is allowed only on a specific day/thread, use that slot.
4. Prefer groups where the topic is naturally relevant: parents of high-school students, school support, local family communities, student/ripetizioni communities.
5. Record each group in a launch sheet with an **internal non-personal identifier** such as `parent_group_01`.

## Posting cadence for the validation

These are operating limits for this launch, not platform rules:

- first wave: **3–5 groups total**, not 20 identical posts;
- one post per group;
- do not repost the same offer into the same group during the 48-hour validation window;
- second wave goes to different groups or uses a genuinely different value angle;
- adapt the first two sentences to the group context when appropriate.

## Transparency

When the context could make the recommendation look neutral, disclose that Sblocco Inglese is your project. Use simple language such as:

> “È un progetto mio, lo segnalo per trasparenza.”

Do not pose as a satisfied parent/student, manufacture testimonials or create fake third-party recommendations.

## Comments and DMs

- Answer public questions where possible.
- Do not send unsolicited sales DMs to people who merely reacted to a post.
- If somebody asks privately, answer the question directly before mentioning the paid path.
- Do not collect or encode member names, emails, children's names or other personal details in UTMs.

## Link rule

The default organic link is the **free diagnostic**, not the checkout or a hard-sell purchase URL.

Use:

`https://sbloccoinglese.com/test-recupero-inglese?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=recupero_august_2026&utm_content=<non_personal_group_identifier>`

Examples:

- `utm_content=parent_group_02_structure`
- `utm_content=student_group_01_priorities`
- `utm_content=ripetizioni_group_02_diagnostic`

Never use a group member's identity in `utm_content`.

# F. What to observe from every group post

Record, by UTM/content ID:

- post approved / rejected;
- clicks / landing visits;
- diagnostic starts;
- diagnostic completions;
- product CTA clicks;
- questions/comments that reveal objections;
- purchases after Commerce GO.

A post with discussion but no diagnostic starts is not automatically a failure: use the questions to tighten the next version. A post that produces irrelevant traffic should not be copied into more groups just because it gets reactions.
