# Recupero Debito Inglese — Small Meta Validation Test

## Role of Meta in this launch

Meta is **secondary** to:

1. organic parent/student groups;
2. high-intent Google Search.

Do not build a large campaign at H30.

For the paid test, target the **adult buyer/guardian**, not a personalised teen audience. Meta limits advertiser targeting options for teens; the cleanest launch structure is therefore adult-buyer creative with a broad adult audience and the free diagnostic as the first destination.

---

## 1. Paid campaign structure

**Campaign name**  
`IT | Meta | Parent | Recupero Debito | Aug26 | Validation`

**Initial budget**  
**€6/day**

**First test window**  
48 hours after full Technical + Commerce GO.

**Maximum first-window spend**  
**€12**

**Audience**

- Italy.
- Adult only.
- Initial commercial hypothesis: set a **minimum age of 30** and keep the audience otherwise broad rather than stacking narrow interest assumptions.
- All genders.
- No teen ad set.
- No youth-interest targeting strategy.
- No Custom Audience built from minors or child-directed data.

The age choice is a launch hypothesis about the likely parent/guardian buyer, not a claim that every buyer will be 30+.

**Destination**  
`/test-recupero-inglese`

**Activation rule**  
Build creative now. Keep paid delivery paused until Technical + Commerce GO. If early organic/Search data shows no product interest at all, Meta remains paused rather than becoming a substitute for product validation.

# 2. Parent paid ad concept A — “Visible structure”

## Hook

**Non serve controllare ogni pagina che ripassa. Serve che ci sia un piano.**

## Visual concept

Editorial split layout:

- left: headline and three short inputs;
- right: cropped, anonymised screenshot of the recovery plan;
- navy background / white typography / orange emphasis;
- no stock-photo “worried parent”; no school-kid illustration.

Visible text:

**Programma della scuola**  
**Data della prova**  
**Priorità reali**

Small label: `Test gratuito · 24 domande · 6–8 min`

## Primary text

Se tuo figlio ha il debito in inglese, il problema non è sempre trovare altro materiale. Spesso è capire **cosa viene prima**.

Recupero Debito Inglese parte dal programma assegnato dalla scuola, dal tempo che resta e da un test gratuito di 24 domande. Il risultato distingue ciò che è già abbastanza solido, ciò che va ripassato e ciò che ha priorità.

Prima di acquistare il percorso potete vedere il punto di partenza.

Il test non predice il voto e il percorso non garantisce il superamento della prova.

## Headline

**Prima capisci cosa va ripassato**

## CTA

**Scopri di più**

## Intended landing

`/test-recupero-inglese`

## Hypothesis tested

Parents respond more strongly to **visible organisation and reduced supervision burden** than to generic “improve your child's English” messaging.

# 3. Parent paid ad concept B — “The programme is long, the days are not”

## Hook

**Il programma è lungo. I giorni che restano no.**

## Visual concept

Minimal three-column editorial graphic:

1. a cropped school-programme checklist;
2. an exam-date/calendar crop;
3. a prioritised plan crop.

Orange line visually connects the three. Navy/cream base. No green or purple.

## Primary text

Quando una prova di recupero si avvicina, ripassare tutto nello stesso modo può diventare un altro problema.

Recupero Debito Inglese mette insieme **programma della scuola, data della prova e aree deboli** per costruire un ordine di lavoro. Lo studente passa poi da recupero guidato a esercizi, modalità scuola e verifica.

Il primo passo è gratuito: 24 domande, circa 6–8 minuti.

Dopo il GO commerciale, il percorso completo costa **€39 una volta sola, senza abbonamento**.

Nessuna previsione del voto. Nessuna garanzia di superamento.

## Headline

**Un piano per i giorni che restano**

## CTA

**Scopri di più**

## Intended landing

`/test-recupero-inglese`

## Hypothesis tested

Parents respond to the **time-allocation problem** and find the €39 one-time structure credible once they have seen the free diagnostic mechanism.

`/test-recupero-inglese`

# 7. Meta stop / continue rules

These are launch operating rules, not universal platform benchmarks.

## Keep paused if

- Technical or Commerce GO has not been given;
- the sales page still contains an H30-prohibited claim;
- organic/Search shows traffic but no meaningful diagnostic engagement and there is no reason to buy more awareness yet.

## Pause during the 48h test if

- destination breaks;
- comments reveal a material misunderstanding caused by the ad;
- traffic is adult but does not start the diagnostic at all;
- commerce is failing after people click through;
- the full €12 test spend produces no useful diagnostic or lower-funnel signal.

## Continue without increasing budget if

- adult traffic starts and completes the diagnostic;
- some users continue to the product CTA;
- comments/questions indicate the mechanism is understood;
- the channel contributes at least a credible lower-funnel signal even before enough purchases exist to judge CAC.

## Do not scale until

- multiple real purchases exist;
- onboarding works;
- first sessions start;
- the observed CAC can be compared against the €10 / €15 / €20 validation hypotheses in `02-offer.md`.
