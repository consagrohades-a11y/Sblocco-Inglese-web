# Recupero Debito Inglese — Positioning & Funnel

**Launch window:** 14–15 August 2026  
**Market:** Italy  
**Price:** €39 one-time  
**Primary acquisition promise:** structure the recovery work around the school programme, the exam date and the learner's actual weak areas.

## 1. Category and position

Recupero Debito Inglese is **not another general English course** and should not be presented as one.

It is a **structured recovery system for Italian high-school students with an English recovery exam**. It starts from three inputs that matter now:

1. what the school actually assigned;
2. how many days remain before the exam;
3. what the student can already do versus what still needs work.

The school programme remains authoritative. The product helps decide **what to work on first and how to move through it**.

### Primary message

> **Non ripassare tutto a caso.**

### Secondary message

> **Parti dal programma della scuola e da quello che ti manca davvero.**

### One-line product explanation

> Recupero Debito Inglese trasforma programma della scuola, data dell'esame e aree deboli in un piano di recupero ordinato, con pratica e verifiche per argomento.

### What makes the offer distinct

The market already contains tutors, language schools and generic revision material. Our useful contrast is not “better English lessons”; it is:

> **meno scelta casuale, più priorità visibile.**

Do not attack private lessons. Position the product as a low-friction system that can be used independently or alongside school support/tutoring.

## 2. Audience A — Student

### Situation

The student has an English recovery exam and often experiences some combination of:

- procrastination because the programme feels too large;
- repeated starts without a clear study order;
- uncertainty about which grammar areas matter most;
- little time left compared with the amount of material;
- textbook revision that feels endless;
- difficulty distinguishing “I recognise this” from “I can actually do this”.

### Job to be done

> “Dimmi da dove partire oggi e cosa viene dopo, senza farmi ripassare tutto nello stesso modo.”

### Student message pillars

**1. Clarity before volume**  
Non serve partire dalla prima pagina del libro. Prima individua priorità, ripasso e aree già abbastanza solide.

**2. School programme first**  
Il percorso non sostituisce ciò che ha assegnato la scuola: parte proprio da quello.

**3. Time changes the order**  
La data della prova serve a decidere cosa deve venire prima.

**4. Work, then check**  
Ogni argomento passa da recupero guidato a pratica, modalità scuola e verifica.

### Tone

Use direct, adult language. Avoid scolding (“hai aspettato troppo”), infantilising reassurance, school-teacher moralising and panic language.

### Student headline territory

- Non ripassare tutto a caso.
- Hai un programma. Ti serve un ordine.
- Prima capisci cosa manca. Poi studia.
- Il libro è lungo. La priorità deve essere corta.

## 3. Audience B — Parent / guardian

### Situation

The parent knows the student has a recovery exam but may not know how to supervise English topic by topic. The concern is usually less “find more content” and more:

- Is there a real plan?
- Is my child working on the assigned programme?
- Are the remaining days being used sensibly?
- Do we need to add many private lessons, or is there a structured first step?
- What exactly am I paying €39 for?

### Job to be done

> “Voglio vedere una struttura concreta senza dover diventare io l'insegnante di inglese.”

### Parent message pillars

**1. Visible organisation**  
Programme, date and priorities are made explicit rather than left as an undefined pile of revision.

**2. School-aligned scope**  
The school programme remains the reference; the product does not invent a substitute syllabus.

**3. Low-risk first step**  
The free diagnostic can be taken before purchase and gives an immediate picture of areas that are solid, need review or are priority.

**4. Clear commercial model**  
€39 once. No subscription.

**5. No false promise**  
The system does not predict a final grade and does not guarantee passing.

### Parent headline territory

- Un piano di recupero, non altre ore di studio senza ordine.
- Prima del prossimo esercizio, serve capire cosa viene prima.
- Il programma della scuola resta il punto di partenza.
- Più struttura. Meno ripasso a tentativi.

## 4. Message hierarchy

Every launch asset should follow this order whenever space permits:

1. **Problem:** “Non ripassare tutto a caso.”
2. **Mechanism:** programme + exam date + actual weak areas.
3. **Free proof step:** 24-question diagnostic, about 6–8 minutes.
4. **Result:** areas grouped into solid / review / priority.
5. **Paid system:** prioritised plan + Recupera + Allenati + Modalità scuola + Verifica + targeted repeat/remediation when an area remains weak.
6. **Price:** €39, pagamento unico, nessun abbonamento.
7. **Boundary:** not a grade prediction; not a pass guarantee.

Do not lead cold traffic with a feature list. Lead with the study-order problem.

## 5. Funnel design and exact CTAs

### Primary cold funnel

**Facebook / Instagram / groups / organic / informational Search**  
→ **Free recovery diagnostic**  
CTA: **“Fai il test gratuito”**

**Diagnostic start screen**  
CTA: **“Inizia il test”**

**Diagnostic completion / result**  
Result message: “Ora sai cosa è già solido, cosa va ripassato e cosa ha priorità.”  
CTA: **“Trasforma queste priorità in un piano”**

**Product explanation**  
CTA: **“Sblocca Recupero Debito — €39”**  
Support line: **“Pagamento unico. Nessun abbonamento.”**

**Authentication / account step, if required by runtime**  
CTA: **“Accedi e continua”**

**Checkout**  
CTA language around checkout: **“Continua al pagamento”**  
Do not add countdowns, fake stock or invented deadlines.

**Post-purchase onboarding**  
CTA: **“Crea il mio piano di recupero”**

**Plan reveal**  
CTA: **“Inizia dalla prima priorità”**

**First session**  
CTA: **“Inizia la sessione”**

### High-intent Google route

For exact high-intent searches such as “recupero debito inglese” or “esame recupero inglese”:

- **Before Technical + Commerce GO:** campaign may be built and submitted, but keep paid delivery paused; organic/free-test traffic can go directly to `/test-recupero-inglese`.
- **After GO:** core product-intent ad groups may land on `/percorsi/recupero-debito`; problem/learning-intent ad groups should continue to land on `/test-recupero-inglese`.

### Why the free test is the preferred cold entry

The test gives the user value before asking for €39, makes the product mechanism tangible and avoids asking a parent or student to trust a new offer based only on claims.

## 6. Language guardrails

### Use

- piano di recupero
- programma della scuola
- data della prova / giorni che restano
- priorità
- aree da ripassare
- pratica
- modalità scuola
- verifica
- ripetizione / recupero mirato sulle aree deboli
- punto di partenza

### Avoid at H30

- “passerai il debito”
- “garantito” / “promosso garantito”
- grade predictions
- “l'AI ti farà passare” or AI-led selling
- “due simulazioni complete” / “due mock exam”
- full cumulative Readiness v2 claims
- claims that the product replaces the school's programme
- invented scarcity or “ultimi posti”
- generic “impara l'inglese velocemente” positioning

---

## 7. Launch routing rule

**Free diagnostic promotion is allowed before paid GO.**  
**Direct purchase promotion is not.**

At H30, the marketing switch is therefore binary:

- **No paid GO:** continue diagnostic-led organic distribution; keep Google/Meta paid purchase delivery paused.
- **Technical + Commerce GO:** activate the prepared high-intent Search campaign and, only as a secondary test, the adult-buyer Meta campaign.
