# Recupero Debito Inglese — Creative Production Plan

Create **exactly four reusable launch assets**. Do not turn the H30 window into a 30-graphic production sprint.

## Shared visual rules

- Sblocco navy + orange.
- Cream/white neutrals are allowed.
- **No purple.**
- **No green.**
- Minimal editorial composition.
- Strong typographic hierarchy.
- Product UI is the proof; decorative illustrations are secondary.
- No childish school icons, stickers, cartoon mascots or “gaming app” look.
- No red panic graphics or fake countdown timers.
- Never show a real learner's name, email, school, diagnostic token or other personal data in captures.
- Do not capture or promote full cumulative Readiness v2 or the simulations area at H30.

---

# ASSET 1 — 30–45 second product screen recording

## Purpose

Show the mechanism in one continuous story:

**diagnostic → result → school programme → plan → session → school mode → verification**

Primary use:

- organic Instagram/Facebook;
- parent-group proof link/video where allowed;
- product landing support after Technical GO;
- Meta parent creative variation after GO.

## Runtime target

**42–44 seconds**

## Opening hook

**Non ripassare tutto a caso.**

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — 0:00–0:03

**Screen:** diagnostic opening screen.  
**Action:** cursor moves toward “Inizia il test”.  
**Overlay:**

> **Non ripassare tutto a caso.**

**Edit:** no logo animation longer than 0.5s. Start on the problem immediately.

### Shot 2 — 0:03–0:08

**Screen:** answer 1–2 diagnostic questions quickly; show “Non lo so” option for one moment if visually useful.  
**Overlay:**

> **24 domande · circa 6–8 minuti**

**Small line:**

> Prima capisci dove sei.

### Shot 3 — 0:08–0:13

**Screen:** diagnostic result. Pan or zoom over the three result columns.  
**Overlay:**

> **Bene · Da ripassare · Priorità**

**Small line:**

> Non è una previsione del voto.

### Shot 4 — 0:13–0:18

**Screen:** onboarding programme selection. Show school-year selection only briefly, then the assigned-topic checklist.  
**Overlay:**

> **Parti dal programma della scuola.**

**Small line:**

> Seleziona solo ciò che ti è stato assegnato.

### Shot 5 — 0:18–0:23

**Screen:** exam-date input → plan reveal / priority list.  
**Overlay:**

> **I giorni che restano decidono cosa viene prima.**

Do not show a fabricated exam date framed as universal urgency. Use a neutral demo date and label the account as demo if needed.

### Shot 6 — 0:23–0:29

**Screen:** open the first recovery session; show the start of the recovery/practice flow.  
**Overlay:**

> **Recupera → Allenati**

**Small line:**

> Un argomento alla volta.

### Shot 7 — 0:29–0:35

**Screen:** Modalità scuola. Show the task format, not a long answer sequence.  
**Overlay:**

> **Poi prova in Modalità scuola.**

### Shot 8 — 0:35–0:40

**Screen:** topic verification/result state. Show a clean completion/check state or the next targeted step.  
**Overlay:**

> **Verifica l'argomento.**

**Small line:**

> Se resta debole, torna nel lavoro mirato.

### Shot 9 — 0:40–0:44

**Screen:** return to diagnostic start/result or a clean navy end card with small product UI crop.  
**Overlay:**

> **Fai il test gratuito.**

**Footer:**

> 24 domande · 6–8 minuti

**Final CTA:**

> **sbloccoinglese.com/test-recupero-inglese**

## Capture requirements

Capture from a clean desktop browser at 100% zoom. Record at 1080p or higher. Hide bookmarks, extensions and personal browser profile UI where possible.

### Screens needed

1. Diagnostic start.
2. One diagnostic question.
3. Diagnostic result.
4. School-topic programme selection.
5. Exam date / plan reveal.
6. Recovery session.
7. Modalità scuola.
8. Topic verification/result.

### Do not capture

- simulations tab;
- cumulative readiness screen;
- current sales-page line about two simulations;
- Stripe checkout before Commerce GO;
- personal learner data.

# Production order

1. Capture the eight clean UI states required by Asset 1.
2. Edit Asset 1 first; it creates most screenshot material for Assets 2–4.
3. Export Asset 2.
4. Export Asset 3.
5. Build the five-slide Asset 4 using the same typography/grid.

This keeps the entire launch creative system to **four assets built from one capture session**.
