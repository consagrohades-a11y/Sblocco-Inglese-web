# Recupero Debito Inglese — Google Search Validation Campaign

## 1. Fresh relevance check — 14 August 2026

A fresh Italian SERP check for phrases such as **“recupero debito inglese”**, **“debito inglese”** and **“esame recupero inglese”** returns tutoring marketplaces and language-school recovery offers. This confirms that the language is commercially relevant and that searchers already encounter paid/help-oriented solutions.

The useful differentiation for Sblocco is not “we also teach English”. It is:

> **programma della scuola + data della prova + priorità reali + piano strutturato**

Broad terms such as generic English courses or learning English are outside the first validation because they mix many intents unrelated to a school recovery exam.

## 4. Match-type recommendation

For H30 validation:

- **Exact match:** yes.
- **Phrase match:** yes.
- **Broad match:** **no at launch**.

Reason: with €9/day and a narrow product, we need to learn from clean high-intent search terms before paying for expansion.

Do not add generic keywords such as:

- corso inglese
- imparare inglese
- inglese online
- lezioni inglese
- esercizi inglese
- grammatica inglese

A relevant long-tail query may contain words such as “corso” or “ripetizioni”, so do not automatically use those individual words as account-wide negatives. Judge the full search term.

## 12. Stop / continue rules

These are **operator rules for this €39 validation**, not universal Google Ads benchmarks.

### Stop or fix immediately

- Search term is clearly unrelated → add the appropriate negative; do not wait for more spend.
- Ad receives relevant clicks but the destination is broken / wrong → pause the affected ad group.
- Paid traffic hits a sales page containing a prohibited H30 claim → pause paid delivery.
- Checkout or entitlement path fails after GO → pause purchase-led paid traffic and send no additional spend into a broken commerce path.

### Investigate before spending more

- **Relevant visits but no diagnostic starts** → ad/landing promise mismatch or first-screen friction.
- **Diagnostic starts but few completions** → test friction, loading, mobile UX or expectation mismatch.
- **Completions but no product CTA clicks** → result-to-offer transition problem, not a keyword problem.
- **Product CTA clicks but no checkout starts** → offer/account/commerce handoff problem.

### Internal spend guardrails

- First 48h Search budget ceiling at €9/day = **€18 total**.
- Do not raise the budget during that window.
- If later cumulative Search spend reaches **€39 with zero checkout starts**, pause and diagnose before spending another product-price equivalent.
- If Search produces relevant diagnostic completions and product CTA clicks but no purchase yet, do not automatically kill the keywords; inspect the lower funnel first.

### Continue

Continue the ad group when:

- search terms match the intended debt/recovery problem;
- users actually start/complete the diagnostic;
- downstream CTA/checkout behaviour shows movement toward purchase.

Do not scale from one isolated sale. Use the first real customer data to establish the baseline.
