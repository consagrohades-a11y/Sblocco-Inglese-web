# Recovery Curriculum v2 — Readiness policy scope

This branch defines the readiness contract only. It does not activate Curriculum v2 readiness in learner-facing runtime.

Activation is intentionally deferred until:

- Curriculum v2 Year 1–3 outcomes are merged and reviewed;
- assessment blueprint and readiness policy are stable;
- pedagogical and technical gap analysis are complete;
- required P0 evidence content and assessment fragments exist;
- per-outcome/per-axis evidence storage exists;
- legacy and v2 readiness have been compared in shadow mode.

The existing `calculateRecoveryReadiness()` implementation therefore remains unchanged on this branch.
